Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SandFlow ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

The SandFlow application itself does not require account registration or personally identifiable information (such as your name, physical address, or phone number) to access standard core functionality.

However, the application integrates third-party services that may automatically collect certain diagnostic and advertising identifiers:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID), unique device identifiers, operating system version, and device model.
  • Usage & Analytics Data: App interaction, crash logs, performance metrics, and general location (at country or city level derived from IP address).

2. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the application. AdMob may collect and process information to personalize ads or serve non-personalized ads in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

For more details on how Google uses data from apps using its services, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Purpose of Processing

We utilize the collected data strictly for:

  • Delivering, maintaining, and improving application features.
  • Displaying relevant in-app advertisements via AdMob.
  • Monitoring app stability, diagnosing crash reports, and preventing fraud.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not operate proprietary back-end servers storing personal data. Data collected by Google services is retained according to Google's standard retention schedules. Users can reset their Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time via device settings: Settings > Google > Ads.

5. Children's Privacy

SandFlow does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

6. Security

We prioritize data protection. All communication between the app and third-party advertising SDKs uses industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS in transit).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
